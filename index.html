TapeDeck

Browse and publish decentralized sites safely. Site files are read and verified straight from the chain, every site gets a safety check when it opens, and the risks are spelled out before you sign or send anything.

macOS · Windows · Linux

The idea

The scariest thing about doing anything on chain is signing the wrong thing and losing your assets. TapeDeck does two jobs: it opens on-chain sites safely, and it puts your own site on chain. On-chain apps are just sites, so when you open one in TapeDeck, every signature and transaction is checked first. A DeWEB site is really an app living in a container (a safe box), so we can also communicate through those containers.

Safety comes first

Content is verified and checked before a site opens, and anything you are asked to sign is put into one plain sentence. Your wallet stays in your own browser extension; TapeDeck never touches your private keys.

Sites live on chain

Site files are stored on the blockchain and never pass through a gateway server. There is no server to renew and no domain to expire. What you see is exactly what the publisher put there.

Publishing takes a few clicks

Pick a folder on your computer, preview it, then publish it on chain in one click, with the cost listed up front. No site yet? Generate one with AI in TapeCode Plus.

No tech skills needed

Checks, publishing and verification are a few fields and a few clicks. TapeDeck handles the on-chain details so you can focus on what you want to do.

What it does

Decentralized sites

A safety check on every open

Every site is checked as it opens: what it will ask you to sign, whether it tries to phish your seed phrase or carries coin-stealing code, and whether the contracts it uses can drain funds. The shield in the address bar shows the result: green is fine, yellow needs a look, red is high risk.

Risks explained before you sign

Transactions are simulated on chain first, so you see how much will actually leave your wallet. Unlimited approvals, approve-all-NFTs, blind signing and address poisoning are explained in one sentence, and the default button is "Reject".

Site permissions

Set rules for each identity: whether wallet signatures are allowed, how much token value can be moved, and whether NFTs can be moved. The rules live in the identity's safe box, and any site request beyond them is blocked.

Safe browsing

Turn it on to record which sites you visited and what your wallet did. When you are done, you decide whether to save the record on chain. If anything ever goes wrong, the record proves whether you visited a site or made a transaction on a given day.

Open on-chain sites

Enter a site number or a wallet address to open sites stored on BNB Chain, X Layer or Base. Content is verified before it opens, so what you see is what the publisher put there.

Following and recent

Follow the sites you like, with a safety check before they are added. Recently visited sites are remembered too, one click away.

Build and publish

Upload and publish

Pick a folder on your computer, preview it, then publish it on chain in one click, with the cost listed up front.

Paid apps

Set a price on your app so only people who paid can use it.

Gateway domain

Activate it once and anyone can open your site in a regular browser, even without TapeDeck.

Wallet site check

Enter a wallet address or a site to check every site it holds, sorted by risk, so problems stand out at a glance.

TapeCode Plus

Open tapecode.ai inside TapeDeck to generate a site with AI, then open the result in a new tab.

Secure on-chain apps

Two-way identity verification

Link your on-chain identity and your X account to each other, so others can confirm the identity really is yours.

Send sealed files

Files are encrypted on your computer and stored on chain; only the people you choose can open them.

Safe box lookup

Enter an identity to see what is in its safe box, no wallet needed.

Paid sealed delivery

Once the buyer pays, the key to the sealed file is handed to them automatically.

Identity and messages

On-chain identity

Own an identity on chain. In your profile you can buy blocks, open safe boxes and manage site permissions.

Encrypted messages

End-to-end encrypted messages between identities; on-chain links in a message open right in TapeDeck.
